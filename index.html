How this works

LocalWork pulls job postings from employers within about a 20-minute drive of ZIP 23185 (Williamsburg, VA), plus fully-remote roles explicitly tied to Virginia (not nationwide "Remote, US" postings), then compares each posting's stated requirements against your resume's skills and tools. Use the Local/Remote checkboxes above the list to filter either category out.

Score = the percentage of a job's listed requirements that match something on your resume. Roles are automatically moved to Not a Fit when a requirement clearly isn't on your resume at all (a clinical license, a commercial driver's license, a physical-labor demand, a required unrelated technical skill, etc.) or when fewer than 40% of requirements match — or when the posting is more than 7 days old. Postings from the last business day get a small score boost (🔥 in the list, noted in the detail view) since a fresher posting is worth applying to sooner.

Most postings are scored by Claude actually reading the full listing and separating real qualifications from job duties/perks — done once when the job data is refreshed, not live in your browser, so there's no API metering or rate limit involved. Postings without that read-through yet fall back to instant on-device keyword/phrase matching (each job's detail view says which method scored it). Neither is a guarantee — always sanity-check a role yourself before applying, and use Save or the tabs above to override anything the scorer got wrong.

Job data is a static snapshot refreshed manually — see README.md / scripts/refresh-jobs.md in the app folder for how to pull a new batch.
